# Hero atmosphere

The mountain background was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The final asset is `frontend/public/hero-mountains.jpg`. The stars are a deterministic, transparent SVG in `frontend/public/hero-stars.svg`.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: panoramic background strip for an existing violet technology landing page. Primary request: a very subtle realistic distant alpine mountain ridge at night. Composition: extra-wide landscape panorama approximately 3:1. Mountain silhouette and naturally weathered rock detail occupy the bottom 35 percent, low rolling peaks, no huge central peak. Upper 65 percent is smooth nearly black plum night sky, no stars (added separately in the website), with a gentle lavender atmospheric haze along the horizon. Color palette: deep violet shadows, muted lavender moonlight on the upper rock edges, dark plum background. Real photographic terrain, restrained contrast, quiet premium atmosphere. No snow-white highlights, no dramatic sunset, no trees, no buildings, no water, no people, no text, no logo, no frame or watermark. Intended to blend subtly into a purple gradient hero, never compete with foreground copy and logo.
